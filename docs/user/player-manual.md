# Vellum Rift — Player Manual

A short guide for people exploring a Space in the browser or on a Meta Quest headset.  
You do **not** need to be a developer to use this.

**Facilitators / classroom setup:** see [classroom-museum-runbook.md](../qa/classroom-museum-runbook.md) and [museum-guest-entry.md](../qa/museum-guest-entry.md).

---

## What is Vellum Rift?

Vellum Rift turns manuscript pages into a shared **3D landscape** you can walk through with others. A **Space** is the place you meet. The **manuscript** is the big model in the middle. You can look around, point a laser so friends see what you mean, and drop **pins** as landmarks.

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Space** | The shared exhibit / room you join |
| **Lobby** | The web page before you enter 3D |
| **Manuscript** | The 3D model built from an uploaded page or image |
| **Laser** | A pointing beam others can see |
| **Pin** | A marker you place on the manuscript |
| **Kiosk / guest** | Join with a nametag — no account required |

## Join on the web (guest — no account)

1. Open the invite link or scan the QR code you were given.  
   It usually looks like a dashboard URL with `session=…` and `kiosk=1`.
2. Type a **nametag** (the name others will see).
3. Click **Enter 3D**.
4. If a new window does not open, click **Open 3D** on the same page (your browser may have blocked a popup).
5. Wait until the manuscript appears.

**Stuck facing the wrong way?** On the join page, click **Respawn me**, then look at the 3D window again.

**Alternate:** On the login page, choose **Join museum exhibit** or **Join as guest**, enter the Space ID posted at your station, then continue as above.

---

## Join on the web (signed-in)

1. Open the Vellum Rift dashboard.
2. Click **Sign in with Bluekey** and finish sign-in.
3. On **Home**, choose **Join a space**, or open **Spaces** and pick your Space.
4. In the **Lobby**, click **Enter 3D** (leave mode on **Play** unless you are running a wall display).
5. Explore with the keyboard and mouse (next section).

Hosts who upload manuscripts and manage playlists should use **Upload**, **Library**, and **Host tools** in the Lobby. Guests do not need those screens.

## Web controls (keyboard and mouse)

Click once inside the 3D view so keys work.

To navigate the space, hold the **right mouse button** and move the mouse to look around. Release when you are done. To move laterally, use the standard **WASD** scheme: **W** moves you forward, **A** strafes left, **S** moves back, **D** strafes right. **Space** moves you vertically up; **Left Ctrl** or **X** moves you down. **Z** and **E** rotate your view left and right. Hold the **left mouse button** to activate a laser pointer aimed with your view. Press **F** to drop a pin. The How to Play and connection status windows are shown and hidden with **H**. If chat is enabled, click the chat box, type your message, and press **Enter** to send it to everyone in the Space. To leave, use **Leave** in the web shell or close the browser tab.

| Action | Control |
|--------|---------|
| Look | Hold right mouse + move |
| Move | W A S D |
| Up / down | Space / Ctrl or X |
| Turn | Z / E |
| Laser | Hold left mouse |
| Place pin | F |
| Show / hide panels | H |
| Send chat (if on) | Click chat → type → Enter |

## Join on Meta Quest

1. Stand inside the taped play area if one is marked. Stay inside the tape while the headset is on.
2. Put on the headset and hold both controllers.
3. Launch the **Vellum Rift** app.
4. Pick today’s exhibit from the event list (or wait if it auto-joins when only one event is open).
5. Wait until the manuscript appears.

**First time tip:** A Menu may open with How to Play. You can close it and open it again anytime (see below).

---

## Quest controls (Touch controllers)

To navigate the space on Meta Quest, push the **left thumbstick** to move; push the **right thumbstick** left or right to snap-turn. Squeeze the **left grip** to jetpack along your look direction. Squeeze the **right trigger** to activate the laser pointer from the right controller. Press **A on the left controller** to place a pin where you are aiming. While aiming at an existing pin, press **A on the right controller** to rename it, or **B on the right controller** to delete it. Press **Y on the left controller** to call for help. Raise your **left wrist** and look at the **MENU** pad to open How to Play and status panels; look at **CLOSE** on the same pad to dismiss them. With the menu open, laser-select **LOG OUT** to leave your seat and return to the event list without ending the shared Space for others.

| Action | Control |
|--------|---------|
| Move | Left stick |
| Snap turn | Right stick |
| Jetpack (along look) | Left grip |
| Laser | Right trigger |
| Place pin | Left A |
| Rename pin | Aim + right A |
| Delete pin | Aim + right B |
| Call for help | Left Y |
| Menu | Raise left wrist → look at MENU / CLOSE |
| Log out (guest) | Menu → LOG OUT |

Some museum Spaces use a simpler **Laser + Jetpack** scheme (host-selected): any left-hand button jetpacks; any right-hand button fires the laser; menu is still look-at-left-wrist.

## Playing well with others

- Point with the **laser** when you want someone to look at a detail.
- Use **pins** for places you want the group to remember.
- Move slowly near the manuscript so others can follow.
- If you feel dizzy or stuck in VR, press **left Y** (Call for help) or take the headset off and ask an adult.
- On Quest, observers stay **outside** any floor tape; only the player stands inside.

---

## Leave

| Client | How to leave |
|--------|----------------|
| **Web** | Leave / exit in the shell, or close the tab |
| **Quest guest** | Wrist MENU → **LOG OUT** (Space stays open for the next person) |

## Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---------|----------|
| 3D window did not open (web) | Click **Open 3D**; allow popups for the site |
| Keys do nothing (web) | Click inside the 3D view, then try again |
| Far from the manuscript (web guest) | **Respawn me** on the join page |
| Quest shows no events | Ask staff to turn **Kiosk on** and mark the Space as an **Event** |
| Need help in VR | Left **Y**, or MENU → Call for help |
| Chat missing | Chat may be turned off for this exhibit — that is normal |
